Travel Thoughtfully

A Few Things to Know Before You Go.

Ladakh is beautiful, but its high altitude and changing weather mean that planning is important.

Best Time to Visit

Many visitors choose late spring through early autumn, but weather and road access can change.

Give Yourself Time

Allow time to adjust to the high altitude before doing strenuous activities.

Ride Responsibly

Check road conditions, wear a helmet and never rush on mountain roads.

Respect the Land

Keep places clean, respect local customs and take your rubbish back with you.
